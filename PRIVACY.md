# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-08_

LinkVault is built to keep your data on your own device. This page explains what that means.

## What LinkVault stores

Your bookmarks, collections, banners and settings (theme, view and sort order) are saved in your browser's **local storage**. They stay on your device.

- LinkVault has **no accounts**, **no server** and **no database**.
- The author of this project **cannot see** your links.
- There is **no analytics**, advertising or tracking built into the app.

## What leaves your device

LinkVault loads some things from other services. Your browser contacts them directly, and they may see your IP address and basic request details under their own policies.

| Service | Why it is used | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **GitHub Pages** | Hosts the site | A normal page request |
| **unpkg, Tailwind CDN** | Load React, Babel and Tailwind CSS | A normal file request |
| **Google favicon service** | Shows a site's logo on its card | The **domain name** of each saved link, such as `github.com` |
| **DuckDuckGo icon service** | Backup source for site logos | The **domain name** of a saved link, only if the first source fails |
| **Unsplash** | Banner photos for a few popular sites | A normal image request |
| **Image links you paste** | Show your custom banners | A request to the site hosting that image |

If you do not want the domains of your links sent to a logo service, you can host your own copy of LinkVault and remove the logo feature.

## Clearing your data

You can remove everything at any time by clearing your browser's site data for LinkVault. This cannot be undone, and there is no copy on our side to restore from.

## Children

LinkVault does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
